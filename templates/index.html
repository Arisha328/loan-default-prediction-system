About the project

A transparent alternative to black-box underwriting

Traditional credit decisions are slow and opaque. LoanAI combines a trained Logistic Regression classifier with plain-language explanations, so loan officers see not just a verdict, but the specific factors driving it — high DTI ratio, low credit score, short employment history, and more.
